# Northstar People Co. Flexible Work Policy

> Fictional demonstration content only. Northstar People Co. and this policy are not real.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed 90 days of service and are meeting role expectations may request a hybrid work arrangement. Roles requiring continuous on-site coverage are not eligible.

## Standard arrangement

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to two days per week. The employee and manager must agree which days are remote, and business coverage takes priority.

## Equipment

The company provides one laptop and one external monitor. This fictional policy does not provide a bicycle, internet, or home-office allowance.

## Review

Managers review hybrid arrangements every six months. HR may suspend an arrangement when security, performance, or operational requirements are not met.
